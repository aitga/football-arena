About Football Arena

Football Arena is an independent football website for fans everywhere. We bring together the latest official highlights and football news from the world's biggest leagues — the Premier League, LaLiga, the Champions League, Serie A, the Bundesliga and more — in one simple place, updated every hour.

What you'll find here

  • Highlights: the newest official highlight videos, played on each league's own YouTube channel.
  • News: headlines from trusted publishers such as BBC Sport, The Guardian and ESPN, always linking to the original story.
  • Articles: our own easy-to-read guides about the rules, tactics and history of football.

Our approach

We respect the work of clubs, leagues and journalists. We never re-upload other people's videos or copy their articles: we link to the official source so the creators get the credit. Our own articles are written by the Football Arena team.
